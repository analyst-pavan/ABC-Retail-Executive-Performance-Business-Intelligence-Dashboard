# 📊 ABC Retail — Executive Performance & Business Intelligence Dashboard

> **An interactive Excel-based Executive Dashboard designed to transform retail sales data into actionable business insights through KPI tracking, trend analysis, regional performance, customer segmentation, and operational reporting.**

---

## 🚀 Project Overview

The **ABC Retail Executive Performance & Insights Dashboard** is an interactive business intelligence solution built in Microsoft Excel to help management monitor overall retail performance and quickly identify important business trends.

The dashboard brings multiple business dimensions into a single executive view, allowing users to analyze:

- 💰 Revenue & Net Sales
- 🛒 Order & Quantity Performance
- 📈 Monthly Revenue Trends
- 🌎 Regional Sales Performance
- 🏷️ Category-wise Sales
- 👥 Salesperson Performance
- 📦 Order Status Distribution
- 🧑‍💼 Customer Type Performance
- 🔄 Order Return %
- 💵 Average Order Value (AOV)

---

# 🎯 Business Objective

The primary objective of this project is to provide management with a **centralized, interactive and decision-focused reporting solution**.

Instead of analyzing raw transactional data across multiple sheets, executives can use the dashboard to quickly answer questions such as:

> **How much revenue are we generating?**

> **Which region contributes the most sales?**

> **Which product categories drive revenue?**

> **How are sales trending month over month?**

> **Which salespersons contribute the highest share of sales?**

> **What is the current order and return situation?**

> **How does performance differ across customer types?**

# 📈 Dashboard Analysis

## 1️⃣ Monthly Revenue Trend

The monthly trend visualization tracks revenue performance from **January to August**, allowing users to identify:

- Monthly growth and decline
- Revenue fluctuations
- Peak-performing months
- Underperforming periods
- Overall sales momentum

---

## 2️⃣ 🌎 Regional Revenue Performance

The regional analysis compares revenue contribution across:

- East
- South
- North
- West

This helps management understand geographic sales distribution and identify regions requiring further investigation.

---

## 3️⃣ 🏷️ Category-wise Sales Performance

The category analysis provides a breakdown of sales across major product categories:

- Bags
- Electronics
- Furniture
- Lifestyle
- Stationery

This enables management to understand which product categories contribute significantly to overall sales.

---

## 4️⃣ 👥 Salesperson Performance

The salesperson performance section compares individual contribution percentages.

This can help management analyze:

- Individual sales contribution
- Performance differences
- High-contribution salespersons
- Sales team distribution

---

## 5️⃣ 📦 Order Status Distribution

Orders are analyzed according to their current status:

- Delivered
- Returned
- Cancelled

This provides visibility into operational order outcomes and helps identify potential areas for process improvement.

---

## 6️⃣ 🧑‍💼 Customer Type Analysis

The dashboard compares **Net Sales by Customer Type**, helping management understand revenue contribution across different customer segments.

---
